Under the Weather

11. 5. 2026

People say they love the rain,
but hold umbrellas in the fear of feeling it on their skin and having it soak into them.

People say they love the sun,
but wear sunscreen so that they avoid burning themselves in its light.

People say they love the snow,
but avoid grasping to it for too long when it starts to hurt from the cold.

People say they love.
